# Menu design

The menu uses a dark command-deck surface with mint accents, large plain typography and subtle moving light. Gameplay keeps its terminal typography.

- Background: #05090D to #09262B, slow shader light.
- Panels: #0B1923; borders #243F4B; text #E0F6EE; secondary #A8BFCB.
- Selected tabs: #214F45 with #69CAAE border.
- Corners: 10px. Spacing: 12px in forms, 20px between groups.
- Motion: 650ms entrance, 280ms tab reveal, cubic ease-out.
- Singleplayer opens solo automatic-wave survival using shared-board rules and one READY action.
- Multiplayer retains Host, Join and local two-player play, with duel/co-op selection.
- Scroll containers handle small windows; Settings, Handbook and Quit remain outside the scrolling content.

Audio remains menu cue in the lobby and original in-game cue during matches.
